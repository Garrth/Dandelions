Terms of Use

Last updated November 12, 2026

Dandelions is a new project, run for now by its founder. These terms explain, in plain words, the deal when you use dandelions.lol. By using the site, you agree to them. How we handle your information is in the privacy policy.

What Dandelions is, and isn't

  • Dandelions is testing a new way to decide what work gets done. Its goals are in the charter.
  • A pledge is not a purchase. Nothing on the site has cash value today. Points aren't turned on yet, and nothing here is an investment.
  • No promises. We don't promise jobs, income, prices, dates, or results. Plans can change, and Dandelions may not work. We'll be honest about how it's going.
  • Dandelions doesn't sell anything on this site and doesn't take payments.

Who can use it

  • Anyone can read the site. To pledge, post, claim a business, or vote, you must be 18 or older.
  • One account per person. Use your own email and keep your login to yourself.
  • Give true information. Some things, like voting, need an ID check so every vote is one real person.

Talk

  • Follow the Talk rules: be constructive, go after ideas not people, no hate, keep it real (no spam, ads, scams, or made-up claims), keep private things private, and stay on topic.
  • What you post in Talk can be read by anyone, along with your first name.
  • You keep ownership of what you write. You let us show it on the site, and let Rai read it to write summaries without names.
  • A post flagged by several members can be hidden until someone looks at it. Moderators can hide posts and pause people who keep breaking the rules. Every moderator action is logged.

The directory

  • Listings start from public data, like Overture Maps and state license lists, so some may be out of date or wrong. Tell us with the report link on the listing.
  • Only the owner, or someone the owner allows, may claim a business. Claiming means confirming a code sent to the business's phone or email. Claiming someone else's business is not allowed, and the claim can be removed.
  • Offers and deals of the day come from the businesses, not from Dandelions. A business must honor its offer as written. Anything you buy is between you and the business.
  • We can remove listings or offers that are false, harmful, or break these terms.

Baselines and voting

  • Members propose changes to the baselines, discuss them in Talk, and vote. One person, one vote. Votes are private.
  • Fake accounts, buying votes, or pressuring others to vote a certain way can get your vote removed and your account closed.

Rai

  • Rai is an AI guide. It can be wrong. Check anything important, and don't treat its answers as legal, tax, medical, or financial advice.
  • Rai doesn't approve, pay, or decide anything. People and the public rules do.

Things not to do

  • Break the law, or use the site to harm, cheat, or harass anyone.
  • Try to get into other people's accounts or data, overload the site, or get around its limits.
  • Copy the directory in bulk to sell it or to send unwanted messages.

The site itself

  • The code is open source under the AGPL-3.0 license, at github.com/Garrth/Dandelions.
  • The site is new and is offered as it is. Parts may break or change, and it may be down sometimes. We aren't responsible for losses from using it, as far as the law allows.
  • We can close an account that breaks these terms. You can close yours anytime by emailing privacy@dandelions.lol.

Changes

If we change these terms, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell members by email first.
