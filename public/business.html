Get found by people who want to buy local.

Dandelions members look for businesses in the Dandelions directory. Your business may already be there. Claim it, make it yours, and post deals. It's all free while we get started.

How it works

  1. Find your business. Search the directory. Listings start from public data, so most businesses are already there.
  2. Claim it. We send a code to the business phone. Enter it and the listing is yours. It takes about a minute.
  3. Make it yours. Add your hours, photos, a short description and an offer. Changes go live right away, with a Verified mark.
  4. Book a deal of the day. Pick a day and post a special offer. It shows on the Dandelions front page that day, one of three deals for your county. Each business can book one day a week.

What makes a good deal of the day

  • Something people would come in for just to try you out, like a free coffee with a pastry or half off a first visit.
  • Clear and simple. Say what it is, the regular price and the deal price.
  • Honor it as written. The deal is between you and your customer.

Where the directory is open

The directory is open in Sonoma County now, and more Bay Area counties are being added. If your county isn't open yet, pledge as a business and we'll let you know when it is.

Later

When points are turned on, you'll be able to accept them from customers. Nothing has cash value today, and listing your business is free.
