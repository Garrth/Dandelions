Put Dandelions on your phone

It takes about 10 seconds. Dandelions gets its own icon on your home screen and opens full screen, like any other app. Nothing to download from an app store.

You're in the app now. You can close this page. Dandelions is on your home screen.

Android phone

  1. Open www.dandelions.lol in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen (or Install app), then Install.

iPhone

  1. Open www.dandelions.lol in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up). On newer iPhones it may be behind the three dots at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Chrome on iPhone works too on iOS 16.4 and newer: Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Good to know

  • Log in once inside the app. On iPhone the app keeps its own log in, separate from Safari. Use your password; email links open in Safari, not the app.
  • Notifications: follow a topic in Talk and you'll get an email when someone posts or comments there. Phone pop-up alerts come next.
  • It's the same site, so it's always up to date. To remove it, press and hold the icon and remove it like any app.
