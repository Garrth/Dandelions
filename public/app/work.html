    How a project works

      A disagreement about a check goes to a random panel of members.

      Approved project categories

      Eight broad kinds of work, for the things the market doesn't pay for. They unlock one at a time as pledges in your county grow.

      The unlock points are starting numbers. Members can change them by vote. Categories rarely change. Projects inside them are added or removed by vote.

      Dandelions doesn't hire anyone

      • Points go to businesses, never to a person. Working on your own? List yourself as a one-person business.
      • Nobody hands out work. Any qualified business can start a project at the set prices, and a random checker approves it.
      • You set your own hours and methods. Dandelions checks results, not people.
      • Nobody checks their own work, or the work of a business they're tied to.
      • Everything is in a public log, so anyone can see what was paid and why.

      Helping early

      People who sign up ID-checked members before Work starts get first pick of projects when they open, if they open. Your share link is on your home page.
