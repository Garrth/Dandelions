As your county grows

  1. NowThe directory, Talk and Rai are open. You can line up for positions, and a few can start early, unpaid.
  2. Starting numbers reachedThe first kinds of work open. Businesses bid on projects and get paid in points once the work is checked.
  3. Sprout, 1% pledgedMore kinds of work open as members vote them in, like caregiving, social events and training.
  4. Bloom, 5% pledgedBigger local projects, more roles and more checkers.
  5. Puffball, 10% pledgedThe county is ready for its biggest projects, and helps nearby counties grow.

At every step, the more people and businesses pledge, the more work can be paid each month.

How a job will work

    Kinds of work

    These open first. Tap one to see a sample project. Members vote in more kinds as the network grows.

    Line up for a position

    Pick the positions you'd like when Work starts. It saves your interest. It isn't a job offer or a promise of work.

    Dandelions doesn't hire anyone

    • Points go to businesses, never to a person. Working on your own? List yourself as a one-person business.
    • Nobody picks the winner by hand. Businesses bid, and a public formula of price and track record decides.
    • You set your own hours and methods. Dandelions checks results, not people.
    • Nobody checks their own work, or the work of a business they're tied to.
    • Everything is in a public log, so anyone can see what was paid and why.

    Helping early

    People who bring in ID-checked members before Work starts get first pick of projects when they open, if they open. Your share link is on your home page.
