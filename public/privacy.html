Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

Dandelions is a new project, run for now by its founder. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect when you pledge at dandelions.lol and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Your email address. We use it to confirm your pledge and to let you sign in.
  • Your ZIP code and the county it's in. We use them to count pledges by county.
  • That you said you're 18 or older, and when.
  • Who referred you, if you came from someone's link.
  • Where you found us, if the link you used was tagged, like one from a video.
  • Which to-do list items you checked, if any. These are counted only after you confirm your pledge.
  • Your profile, if you fill it in: a display name, your town, a photo, your skills and the hourly rate you'd want for each, whether you're open to work, and certifications. Your answers about your current work (how many jobs, hours, and a pay range) stay private and are only ever counted in totals.
  • What you post in Talk: posts, comments, +1s, flags, the topics you star, and the people you follow.
  • Baseline proposals and votes. Your vote is stored so you can change it until the vote closes, and so each person votes once.
  • If you claim a business: the business phone number or work email the code was sent to, and a record of the claim.
  • If you do the ID check: only whether it passed, and when. See below.
  • Basic technical records our providers keep to run the site, like the time you signed in.

We don't collect your home address or any payment information. We don't use advertising trackers.

The ID check is done by Plaid, through an account run by Equitide. You show your ID to Plaid, not to us. We only get back whether the check passed. We never see or keep a copy of your ID.

Ideas for the to-do list are saved without your name or email. Please don't put personal information in them.

Ask Rai chats are sent to Anthropic, which makes the Claude AI, so it can write Rai's answers. We don't save chats. If Rai can't answer a question, we save that one question and the page you were on, without your name, email or address, so we can teach Rai the answer. We remove email addresses and phone numbers from it first, and delete it after 30 days. To keep Rai within its daily limits, we count questions using a scrambled code made from your internet address. The address itself isn't saved.

What we show publicly

  • Counts: how many people have pledged in each county, and how many checked each to-do list item.
  • Your Talk posts and comments, with your first name. Anyone can read Talk.
  • Your profile only if you set it to visible, and then only to members who have passed the ID check. It's private unless you change that.
  • Baseline proposals, with the first name of whoever proposed them. Votes are never shown one by one, only the totals when a vote closes.
  • Business listings and what owners add to them.

We never show your email. Who you follow is private, and nobody can see a list of who follows them.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with advertisers. We use a few services to run the site, and they handle your data only to do that job:

  • Supabase stores the database and handles sign-in.
  • Resend sends our emails.
  • Anthropic writes Rai's answers. Under its terms for this kind of use, it doesn't train its AI on these chats.
  • DigitalOcean hosts the website.
  • Twilio sends the code by text or phone call when someone claims a business.
  • Plaid, through Equitide's account, does the ID check.
  • jsDelivr delivers one piece of code the site uses to talk to Supabase.

We would share information if the law requires it.

Emails

Right now we only send the emails you ask for, like your confirmation link. If we ever start sending updates, each one will have a way to unsubscribe.

Age

Dandelions is for people 18 and older. If we learn someone under 18 has pledged, we'll delete their information.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you what we have about you, correct it, or delete it. Email privacy@dandelions.lol from the address you pledged with and we'll take care of it.

Security

Your data is stored with database rules that keep each member's record private. No system is perfect, so we collect as little as we can.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is important, we'll tell members by email first.
