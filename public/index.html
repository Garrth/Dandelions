Dandelions so far

The problem

AI is taking over more of the work people do, and nobody has a real plan for what comes next. Meanwhile the work we need most, like clean energy, real food, healthcare and job training, keeps waiting on one question: who will pay for it?

The Dandelion solution

Dandelions is a network of neighbors and local businesses. It has its own way to pay for the work that matters, it's run by the people in it with the help of AI, and it's built on one belief: we can make life better while putting back more than we take.
