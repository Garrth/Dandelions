    The work is there. The system isn't.

    The people are here. The materials are here. The work is right there on the list. What's missing is a system that puts them together.

    Today, work mostly gets done when it pays back fast. Most of this list pays back slowly, or in ways nobody can send a bill for, like cleaner water or healthier soil. So it waits, no matter how many people are ready to do it.

    Dandelions is testing a different way to decide what gets done. The more people take part, the closer we get.

    How it grows

    One county at a time.

    Seed

    The first pledge plants your county on the map.

    Sprout

    1% of the county has pledged. That starts phase 2: small local projects you can choose to join.

    Bloom

    5% have pledged. The county is ready for the next step.

    Puffball

    10% of the county. It sends seeds to the counties next door.
