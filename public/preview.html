Dandelions Pledge

What if money paid for the work we actually need?

Today, money is lent into existence. Only work that can pay back a loan with interest gets funded. Dandelions is building a different kind of money: created debt-free, only when real work gets done, for things like housing, healthcare, care work, and restoring the planet.

Where things stand today

We're at the very beginning. There are no tokens yet, no way to earn them, and nothing to buy. Nobody is asking you for money.

Right now we're counting people. When you pledge, you're saying: "If Dandelions money exists in my area, I'll accept it." That's it. Each pledge shows up as a count for your area, so we can see where enough people care to get started.

How an area grows

  1. Seed You pledge. Your area gets its first count.
  2. Sprout Neighbors and local businesses pledge too.
  3. Bloom The area reaches its local goal and is ready for the next step.
  4. Seed head Members spread the word to the next area.

What happens at "bloom" will be decided in the open, with members. We'll tell you plainly as it takes shape.

What we believe

  • A job that pays a living wage should be there for anyone who wants one. For some people, that job is simply getting better.
  • We should put back more than we take from the planet.
  • No one gets to print money. It only comes from verified work.
  • Everything happens in the open: the rules, the data, and the code.
  • We won't make promises we can't keep.

Dandelions sits alongside the regular economy. It doesn't replace it.
